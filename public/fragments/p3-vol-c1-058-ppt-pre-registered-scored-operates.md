# Thesis stub — ppt pre registered scored operates

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
The PPT is a pre-registered, scored protocol that operates on paired trials as follows: Setup: For any candidate assumption-exposure move (AEM), construct a matched OAR by taking the same criticized premise and generating a standard objection (e.g., “Premise X is unjustified because…”) followed by a reply that rephrases the premise in light of the objection.

## kill_condition
Thus, the PPT produces a verdict that assumption-exposure fails to generate progress (it merely re-entrains the premise under ecological plausibility), while ordinary OAR produces a measurable distinction.
