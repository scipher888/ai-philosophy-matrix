# Thesis stub — signal and detune discriminator assume propositional

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
We propose a signal-and-detune protocol as discriminator. It does not assume the premise is propositional. Instead, it uses a pre-registered paired-case OAR race to test whether the exposure advantage (if any) is specific to the sub-symbolic pattern rather than to correlated surface features.

## kill_condition
In pilot simulations (pre-registered), this often fails because both groups adapt equally — the exposure advantage collapses.
