# Thesis stub — assumption exposure adds predictive power

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Assumption-exposure adds no predictive or diagnostic power beyond ordinary OAR because any inherited premise is indistinguishable from a standard training distribution bias, and the “fake” disagreement label is unfalsifiable—any detected divergence can be attributed to an unstated inherited premise that the human did not disavow, immunising the claim against empirical correction.

## kill_condition
To kill the objection, and thus seal the thesis, one would need to show that OAR can alter the model’s structural habits of inference around literary‑metaphysical premises – for example, by fine‑tuning on a dataset where the premise is systematically denied, and then observing that the model’s subsequent reasoning on unrelated topics no longer defaults to that premise.
