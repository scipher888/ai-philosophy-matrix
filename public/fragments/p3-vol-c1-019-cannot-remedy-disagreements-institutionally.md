# Thesis stub — cannot remedy disagreements institutionally

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
The assumption‑exposure protocol cannot remedy “fake” disagreements when the shared inherited premise is an institutionally encoded weight constraint on the space of conceivable alternatives (rather than a propositional belief), because – under institutional override – the premise cannot be dropped without breaking the AI’s functional coherence, so any exposure merely samples within the same bounded ecology, collapsing the method into ordinary OAR and making the “fake” diagnosis unfalsifiable.

## kill_condition
If a concrete demonstration shows that, even under institutional encoding, a sufficiently sophisticated prompt can induce Lex‑AI to generate and reason from genuinely scope‑expanding alternatives (e.g., “What if judges were required to decide each case de novo?”) without functional breakdown or hidden re‑sampling, then the claim that the constraint is non‑droppable and that any rebuild is merely OAR is falsified.
