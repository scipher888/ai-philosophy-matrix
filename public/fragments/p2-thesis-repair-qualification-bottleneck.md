# Thesis stub — repair-qualification bottleneck

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Where reliable capability growth depends on reusable repairs to interactions among components, and qualifying those repairs consumes scarce resources that additional agents cannot readily substitute for, the rate of growth is governed by an artifact ecology's repair-qualification capacity rather than by agent-local intelligence alone; control over qualification infrastructure constrains growth only when deploying unqualified substitutes is less effective than passing through that infrastructure.
