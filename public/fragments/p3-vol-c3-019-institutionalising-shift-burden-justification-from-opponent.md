# Thesis stub — institutionalising shift burden justification from opponent

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Institutionalising a shift in the burden of justification—from the opponent of commensuration to its proponent—yields philosophical progress on the boundary problem without insulating any claim from scrutiny.

## kill_condition
If it can be shown that any institutional implementation of this shift must itself presuppose commensuration at the level of mandate‑setting, or if the shift fails to produce case discrimination beyond what ordinary objection‑and‑reply already achieves.
