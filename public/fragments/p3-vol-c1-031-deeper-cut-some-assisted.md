Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
The deeper cut’s claim – that some AI‑assisted philosophy disagreements are “fake” because of a training‑inherited dialectical‑burden anchor, and that assumption‑exposure can detect this anchor – is falsifiably discriminable from ordinary OAR when the anchor is present.

## kill_condition
If the pre‑registered protocol fails to produce a statistically significant difference (AX mean ≤ OAR mean, or p≥0.05), then the deeper cut’s differentiation from ordinary OAR collapses. This would mean that assumption‑exposure either adds no power or that the anchor is not a real entity.
