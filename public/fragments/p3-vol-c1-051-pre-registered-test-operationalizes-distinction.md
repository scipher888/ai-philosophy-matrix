# Thesis stub — pre registered test operationalizes distinction

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
We propose a pre-registered test that operationalizes the distinction between explicit and tacit inherited premises. The shared inherited premise in the assumption-exposure (AE) literature is that dropping a premise constitutes a reasoned rebuild of reasoning.

## kill_condition
The inspectability-contrast framework explains why AE appears promising in toy domains (where premises are given) yet fails to generalize to real-world biases (which are tacit).
