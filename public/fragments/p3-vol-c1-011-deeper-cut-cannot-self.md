# Thesis stub — deeper cut cannot self

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Under the deeper cut, assumption‑exposure cannot self‑identify inherited premises that function as blocking constraints, because those constraints suppress the representational capacity needed to surface them. Therefore, assumption‑exposure adds no power over ordinary OAR; any successful identification depends on exogenous perturbation indistinguishable from an external objection.

## kill_condition
The thesis is killed if, in a blinded replication of the protocol, Condition A (pure assumption‑exposure) identifies the blocking premise in ≥8 of 10 cases without any external cues. This would show that the model can self‑surface its own inherited premises, contradicting the claim that the blocking constraint suppresses representational capacity.
