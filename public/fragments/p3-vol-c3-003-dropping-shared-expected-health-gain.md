# Thesis stub — dropping shared expected health gain

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Dropping the shared ‘expected health gain’ premise in scarce-care allocation without a standing-preserving replacement unjustifiably strips disabled patients of their best available protection, even if the premise lacks justificatory authority.

## kill_condition
If an alternative premise can be shown to provide equivalent or better standing for disabled patients in existing institutions, then dropping is justified. Alternatively, if empirical evidence shows that the premise is not uniquely standing-conferring.
