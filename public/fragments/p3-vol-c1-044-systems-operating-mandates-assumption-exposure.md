# Thesis stub — systems operating mandates assumption exposure

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
In AI systems operating under institutional mandates, assumption-exposure can reveal a shared inherited premise that grounds a fake philosophical disagreement, but the protocol cannot achieve resolution because the premise is weight-encoded as a non-negotiable constraint, making the “drop” step unavailable—and this limitation is not addressed by the deeper cut’s focus on register anchors, because the anchor itself is institutionally enforced.

## kill_condition
If an AI with an explicit institutional mandate (e.g., a legal advisor with a core objective to uphold constitutional supremacy) successfully drops that premise after assumption-exposure and the disagreement is resolved (e.g., both parties adopt a new framework), then the thesis is falsified.
