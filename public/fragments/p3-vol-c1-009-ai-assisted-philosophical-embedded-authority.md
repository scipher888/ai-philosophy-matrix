# Thesis stub — ai assisted philosophical embedded authority

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
In any AI-assisted philosophical disagreement embedded in an institutional authority relation, assumption-exposure can identify but cannot drop a shared inherited premise that functions as a coordinative protocol, because the premise is partially constitutive of the authority relation itself.

## kill_condition
The thesis would be killed by a controlled experiment in which an AI-assisted philosophy disagreement under formal institutional authority undergoes assumption-exposure and achieves sustained premise deletion: at least five turns in which the target term is not reimported, the disagreement remains intelligible, and the final result is a propositional resolution rather than a compliance declaration or an inarticulate rebuild.
