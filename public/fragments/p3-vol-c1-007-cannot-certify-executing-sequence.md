# Thesis stub — cannot certify executing sequence

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Claim: Assumption‑exposure cannot certify a disagreement as “fake” without executing a sequence of iterative objection‑reply steps that is functionally identical to ordinary OAR. Therefore, the concept of “fake disagreement” via inherited premises is either unfalsifiable or reducible to ordinary OAR; the proposed protocol will show that the verdict divergence between the two procedures is not greater than chance, and any apparent additional power is an artifact of procedural noise.

## kill_condition
Kill condition: If, in our paired test, we observe a statistically significant divergence (>2 out of 10) that cannot be attributed to procedural noise (i.e., re‑running ordinary OAR with different seeds never produces the same verdict change), then assumption‑exposure provides a genuine diagnostic advantage.
