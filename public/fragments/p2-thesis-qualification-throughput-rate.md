# Thesis stub — qualification throughput rate

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Under a saturated queue of reusable interaction repairs, one non-substitutable qualification resource, no competitive unqualified route, and no slower downstream stage, mean reliable-capability growth is approximately (Q−M)/a, where Q is qualification capacity, M is maintenance qualification load, and a is prospective qualification work per additional reliable-capability unit; agent-local intelligence changes this rate only by changing these quantities or ending the bottleneck regime.

## kill_condition
Repair-library ablation negligibly affects subsequent growth; qualification expands freely with copied agents/compute; private or unqualified routes compete; preregistered ecological forecasts fail vs agent-local baselines; or the relevant arguments already include this mechanism.
