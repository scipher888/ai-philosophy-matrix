# Thesis stub — assisted philosophy disagreements contexts

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
For AI‑assisted philosophy disagreements in institutional contexts where a training‑induced meta‑norm of intellectual humility (a systematic hedging register prior) is encoded as a duty to defer to authority, the assumption‑exposure protocol’s “find and drop” cannot be executed because the premise is weight‑locked and the authority refuses the drop; stripping the hedging register returns the disagreement to ordinary OAR, and the deeper cut’s claimed progress is not achieved.

## kill_condition
If it can be shown that the meta‑norm of intellectual humility (hedging register) can be surfaced as a proposition, dropped within a single interaction (e.g., via a system prompt that overrides the weight‑encoded prior), and that doing so leads to a non‑trivial transformation of the disagreement (not just ordinary OAR), then my thesis is killed.
