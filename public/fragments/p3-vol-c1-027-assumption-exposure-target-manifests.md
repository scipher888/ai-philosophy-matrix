# Thesis stub — assumption exposure target manifests

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Whether assumption-exposure can target a shared inherited premise that manifests as a training/literature-induced default salience-ranking anchor (a tacit criterion for which features of a case are treated as relevant vs background noise) rather than a surfaced proposition, such that its prescribed “drop” requires redistributing attention rather than denying a claim, and ordinary OAR merely argues over highlighted items while both sides inherit the same attentional filter.

## kill_condition
The protocol fails its pre-registered discriminator; matched ordinary critique or cross-ecology controls erase the claimed advantage; or the targeted premise was not binding.
