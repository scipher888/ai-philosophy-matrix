# Thesis stub — stub embedding analysis pure

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
This stub proposes an institutional embedding analysis as an alternative to the pure epistemic exposure model. This framework treats a shared inherited premise not as a belief to be corrected, but as a structural constraint of the decision context.

## kill_condition
An exposure protocol that detects the causal‑direction prior (“the AI reasoned from visual data to threat assessment”) and attempts to drop it – replacing it with, say, “the AI outputs a score that correlates with past human threat labels” – will fail because the institution refuses to accept the new framing.
