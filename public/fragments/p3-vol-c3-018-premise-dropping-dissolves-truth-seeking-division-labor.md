# Thesis stub — premise dropping dissolves truth seeking division labor

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Premise-dropping dissolves a truth-seeking division of labor iff the successor mistakes the premise's content for its standing-conferring function; a warranted reconstruction separates finitude from fixedness, re-locates the old complaint-types to the successor decision level, yields justified separations among successor options, and grants standing to the complaint the old premise silenced.

## kill_condition
Kill if a case is found where a premise-drop re-grounds all old complaint-types, gives standing to the previously silenced complaint, and yields genuine justified separations at the successor level, yet the division of labor still collapses in a way traceable to the premise-drop itself (rather than to an honest convergence of the two principles).
