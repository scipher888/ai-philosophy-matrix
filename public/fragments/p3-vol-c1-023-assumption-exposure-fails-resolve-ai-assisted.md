# Thesis stub — assumption exposure fails resolve ai assisted

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Assumption-exposure fails to resolve AI-assisted philosophy disagreements when the shared inherited premise is institutionally locked, because the protocol’s “drop” step is unavailable, and the diagnosis of “fake disagreement” collapses into unfalsifiable speculation about training origins.

## kill_condition
If one could show that the institutional authority itself is based on a training-inherited premise, and that exposure could lead to a higher authority (e.g., democratic deliberation) overriding the lower authority, then the protocol might still work via meta-exposure.
