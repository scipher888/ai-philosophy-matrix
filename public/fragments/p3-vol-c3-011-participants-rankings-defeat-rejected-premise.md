# Thesis stub — participants rankings defeat rejected premise

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
The participants’ rankings do not defeat their rejected premise; they expose a distinct tacit premise (hardship → learning potential) that can be isolated via substitution audit; premise-drop is incomplete, not defeated.

## kill_condition
If substitution audit shows no difference between hardship and sympathy-matched non-learning conditions, or if participants reject the audit’s case variation as irrelevant.
