# Thesis stub — separates genuine assumption exposure oar

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
We propose a protocol that separates genuine assumption-exposure from ordinary OAR exploration before any case analysis. The protocol consists of two paired runs under matched inferential resources and evidence, followed by a checklist.

## kill_condition
If a concrete case can be produced where assumption-exposure targeting a scope constraint (e.g., the “agent-like” framing) yields an alternative not reachable by OAR under the same evidence (e.g., a non-agentic model of AI that is not just a minor tweak), then the test fails.
