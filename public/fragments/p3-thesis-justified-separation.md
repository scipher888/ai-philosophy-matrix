# Thesis stub — justified separation

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
For AI-assisted disagreements governed by a shared decision-sufficiency premise, premise-dropping delivers a distinctive representational gain only through justified separation: a rebuilt account warrants different rankings in paired cases that every account retaining the premise must rank alike; attributing an additional methodological advantage requires more such separations than equally resourced, unrestricted objection-and-reply produces.

## kill_condition
Unrestricted objection-and-reply matches or exceeds exposure on warranted held-out separations at equal cost; participants already respond to dependence probes; translating new terminology removes every claimed difference; the intervention confounds with new evidence/objectives/resources; controlled evidence favors reward-induced conformity over inherited framing; or the target debate already tracks the omitted relation.
