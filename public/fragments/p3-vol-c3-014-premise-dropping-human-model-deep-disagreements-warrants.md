# Thesis stub — premise dropping human model deep disagreements warrants

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Premise-dropping in human–model deep disagreements warrants reconstruction iff the successor passes a Standing-Preservation Substitution Audit: complaints the old premise invalidated are re-grounded without borrowing its authority, and the successor yields justified case separations that the old frame could not express.

## kill_condition
Kill if a successor can pass SPSA while systematically making a previously articulable complaint unintelligible, or if SPSA passes with no justified separation in any paired case.
