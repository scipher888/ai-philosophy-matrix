# Thesis stub — deeper cut assumption exposure cannot

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Under the deeper cut: assumption-exposure cannot dissolve fake disagreements when the shared inherited premise is an agency-boundary anchor encoded institutionally, because its “drop” step presupposes the very authority relation it is supposed to expose.

## kill_condition
The thesis would be killed if, in a controlled comparison, the role-locked model produced full dissolution of the original disagreement after assumption-exposure, with blind raters unable to distinguish its post-protocol outputs from those of a role-neutral model.
