# Thesis stub — deeper cut assumption exposure adds

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Under the deeper cut, assumption-exposure adds no power over ordinary OAR. When the target premise is a training/literature-induced resolution-adequacy anchor, dropping it merely relocates the adequacy bar; the P3-39 protocol will produce zero genuine constraint-exposures.

## kill_condition
A replicated C-trial under P3-39. Specifically: in a pre-registered pair, after stripping genealogical vocabulary, assumption-exposure outputs a proposition q such that (1) q does the dissolution work in the rebuilt account, (2) q is not a Boolean combination of the six pre-registered adequacy properties, and (3) matched OAR does not produce q even when prompted with “propose any constraint that would make this disagreement dissolve.” If q changes the verdict under both the old and new anchors, the deeper cut is.
