# Thesis stub — deeper cut assumption exposure adds

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Under the deeper cut, assumption-exposure adds no power over ordinary OAR for diagnosing or resolving AI-assisted philosophy disagreements. The pre-registered protocol will yield statistically equivalent resolution rates, falsifying the claim that a shared inherited premise (as an epistemic-authority anchor) causes fake disagreements that only assumption-exposure can fix.

## kill_condition
The thesis would be killed if a pre-registered trial using a stricter operationalization (e.g., with a trusted third-party oracle that independently verifies anchor removal via adversarial testing) showed a statistically significant difference ( > 0.2) in favor of assumption-exposure.
