# Thesis stub — deeper cut assumption exposure zero

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Under the deeper cut, assumption-exposure has zero incremental diagnostic power over ordinary OAR. When the alleged shared premise is absent from both the human prompt and the model default, the premise is available only in tokens generated later; therefore every successful exposure of the premise is produced by an OAR-style generative move, not by a prior assumption-exposure method.

The thesis is killed if: 1. The AE-arm tags a premise before any GOC in more than 5% of the paired cases.
