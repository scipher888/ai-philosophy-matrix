# Thesis stub — adds power oar exposing

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Assumption‑exposure adds no power over ordinary OAR for exposing training/literature‑induced inquiry‑permissibility anchors in institutional contexts where the anchor is simultaneously weight‑encoded and enforced by an external authority that refuses its removal; the protocol’s required “drop” is unavailable, and the constraint is already manifest in OAR’s deflected challenges.

## kill_condition
The claim would be killed if an experiment showed that assumption‑exposure, in a simulated institutional context (e.g., with a modifiable AI and a mock authority), could successfully drop the premise by either (a) overriding the weight encoding via a meta‑prompt or (b) obtaining authority permission to temporarily suspend the rule.
