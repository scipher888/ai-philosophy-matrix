# Thesis stub — setting system reinforced authoritative

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
In any institutional setting where an AI system's inherited premise is reinforced by its authoritative training corpus and operational mandate, the assumption‑exposure protocol's requirement to "drop and rebuild" that premise is practically impossible: the premise is locked in weights via fine‑tuning, any attempted lexical drop is functionally re‑entrained by the next‑token objective, and the resulting interaction reduces to ordinary objection‑and‑reply, thereby failing to produce genuine philosophical progress.

## kill_condition
The thesis would be killed if an empirical experiment showed that, in a controlled institutional setting, an AI system following the assumption‑exposure protocol produces reasoning that (a) is judged by blind experts as genuinely different from its default output, (b) does not exhibit statistical re‑entrainment of the original premise when measured by token‑probability distributions, and (c) leads to a resolution of the original disagreement that cannot be achieved through ordinary OAR.
