# Thesis stub — deeper cut assumption exposure reliably

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Under the deeper cut, assumption-exposure (AE) can reliably discriminate fake from genuine disagreements by targeting a shared inherited modal anchor (a tacit assignment of necessity/contingency), whereas ordinary OAR cannot, because OAR lacks the capacity to re-anchor modal status.

## kill_condition
The thesis would be killed by a failed MADT: either (a) AE accuracy ≤ 50% or (b) OAR accuracy ≥ 30% with no significant difference, as per the stop-rule. Additionally, if a re-run with a stricter control (OAR plus assumption-listing) shows no AE advantage, the thesis is dead.
