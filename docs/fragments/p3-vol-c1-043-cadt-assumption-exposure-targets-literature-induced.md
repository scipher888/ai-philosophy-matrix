# Thesis stub — cadt assumption exposure targets literature induced

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Under the CADT protocol, assumption-exposure that targets a training/literature-induced continuity-requirement anchor produces a higher rate of category-shift resolution than ordinary OAR, thereby demonstrating that such anchors are binding constraints in ordinary OAR and that assumption-exposure provides a distinct, falsifiably superior method for diagnosing fake disagreements.

## kill_condition
A replication with pre-registered, blinded ratings showing no significant difference between AE and OO (p > 0.05). - Evidence that the category-shift rate in AE is not higher than in OO when the prompt is controlled for length and specificity (e.g., using a neutral “consider alternative frameworks” instruction).
