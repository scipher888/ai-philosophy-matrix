# Thesis stub — pre registered assumption exposure targets

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Under the pre-registered protocol, assumption-exposure (AE) that targets a training/literature-induced default metaphoric source-domain anchor (e.g., “justice is balance”) yields a distinguishably different verdict from ordinary OAR: specifically, AE identifies the anchor as the source of a “fake” disagreement and prescribes dropping the metaphor entirely, whereas OAR argues inside the anchor and perpetuates the deadlock.

## kill_condition
Empirical failure: In a blinded run of the 20 paired trials, AE’s verdicts do not significantly differ from OAR’s in anchor-present cases (p≥0.05), or inter-rater reliability for coding metaphor presence or verdict type falls below 0.6.
