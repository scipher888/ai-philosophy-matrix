## claim
This stub proposes the Assumption‑Coupling Discrimination Protocol (ACDP). The protocol operates before any narrative framing. The shared inherited premise in the seed‑P3 literature (and in many debiasing, reframing, or “x‑risk reducible” programs) is: P1: Assumption-exposure constitutes genuine progress when it removes a previously unresolved disagreement by rendering one party’s position untenable or by narrowing the possibility space.
