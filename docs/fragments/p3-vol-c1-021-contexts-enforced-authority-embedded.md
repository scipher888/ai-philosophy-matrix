# Thesis stub — contexts enforced authority embedded

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
In institutional contexts where a premise is enforced by authority and embedded in model weights as a default burden-of-proof allocation (rather than a surfaced proposition), the “find and drop” protocol is inapplicable because the premise lacks propositional form and the drop is not actionable; therefore assumption-exposure adds no power over ordinary OAR (Objection–Answer–Response) and the “fake” diagnosis is unfalsifiable.

## kill_condition
If someone demonstrates a concrete institutional case where a tacit burden-of-proof allocation can be successfully exposed and dropped without changing weights or institutional rules – for example, by showing that the allocation is actually a proposition that can be stated and voluntarily abandoned by both parties, and that the authority does not actually enforce it – then the thesis would be killed.
