# Thesis stub — assumption exposure nonredundant deeper cut

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Assumption-exposure is nonredundant under the deeper cut: dropping training/literature-inherited agent-boundary anchors to a null ecology before role-negotiation changes verdict or disagreement-status in at least 10 of 40 paired cases against matched OAR; that difference is falsifiable, and the “fake disagreement” diagnosis is not a restatement of ordinary OAR role-negotiation.

## kill_condition
The thesis is killed if: - AE never diverges from OAR+surface in the pre-registered 10/40 cases; - OAR+surface diverges from OAR at the same rate and on the same cases as AE; - AE-explicit diverges at the same rate as AE, showing inheritedness is not binding; - more than 8/40 AE outputs are scored subject-change or no-original-verdict, making the fake label unfalsifiable; - the admission test cannot be applied without post hoc judgments, so that “fake” is decided by the scorer rather than by the protocol.
