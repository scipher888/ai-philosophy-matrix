# Thesis stub — assumption exposure cannot coherently target

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Assumption-exposure cannot coherently target the training-induced scope-boundary anchor in institutional override cases, because its own criterion for which disagreements are legible to fake-diagnosis presupposes that “drop-able” commitments are the relevant kind of commitment — and that is exactly the inherited premise the deeper cut would drop.

## kill_condition
This thesis is killed if a controlled experiment shows that counterfactual-drop assumption-exposure yields stable, non-OAR classifications in institutional override cases. For example: take fifty AI-assisted disagreements in which a hard-coded institutional default conflicts with a philosophical objection.
