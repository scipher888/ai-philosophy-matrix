# Thesis stub — constraints protocols fail resolve

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Under institutional constraints, assumption‑exposure protocols fail to resolve AI philosophy disagreements because the inherited premise is often a weight‑encoded mandate that cannot be dropped without system failure, making the “find and drop” step inoperative and the resulting “rebuild” meaningless – demonstrating that the protocol assumes a fictional autonomy from institutional authority and that the deeper cut’s reduction to sequential priming bias is both unfalsifiable and orthogonal to the real binding constraint.

## kill_condition
If a concrete counter‑example can be provided where an institutional mandate premise (e.g., “follow orders”) is successfully dropped within an assumption‑exposure protocol while respecting institutional constraints – for instance, by renegotiating the premise with the authority or by showing that the premise was not truly necessary for system function – and the rebuild yields a better resolution, then the thesis is killed.
