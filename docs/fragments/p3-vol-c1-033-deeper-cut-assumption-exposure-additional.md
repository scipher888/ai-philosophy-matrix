# Thesis stub — deeper cut assumption exposure additional

Public fragment only — not a contest essay.

## claim
Under the deeper cut, assumption-exposure has additional power over ordinary OAR only if a shared, non-nameable source-admissibility gate—common to the prompt’s literary framing and the model’s training prior—can be toggled without being propositionally stated, causing AI-assisted disagreement to dissolve at a rate OAR cannot match.

## kill_condition
The thesis is killed by any of the following observations: - OAR, within five elicitation passes, reproduces every AE flip. Then assumption-exposure adds no power over ordinary OAR.
